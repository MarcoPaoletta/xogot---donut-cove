# Build notes

Every decision, tuned dial, final light value, final scale, pad price and the measured time of each purchase. One line per item, newest at the bottom.


* M0. Font: Lilita One (OFL, Google Fonts), `assets/fonts/LilitaOne-Regular.ttf`, set as `gui/theme/custom_font`. Chosen for chunky rounded digits that read at a distance; Fredoka and Baloo 2 were the alternatives downloaded.
* M0. `Audio` autoload is a scene (`scenes/core/audio.tscn`): a Music player and a pool of 12 one shot players authored as nodes. Sounds are looked up by file name, so gameplay code is written before the sound files exist.
* M0. Buses: Master, Music, SFX (`default_bus_layout.tres`). Master is muted in `Audio._ready`; `M` toggles. Verified in a run: muted at boot `true`, after `M` `false`.
* M0. The saved mute state is not restored at boot: the boot mute rule wins. `user://settings.json` keeps fullscreen and best time.
* M0. Extra dev action `concept_cam` (Tab) for the concept cameras, removed before the final build.
* M1. Tycoon scripts adapted from the tycoon-juice reference and written through xo as project scripts in `scripts/core/` (`ItemFlight`, `CarryStack`, `ItemTray`, `PopNumber`, `ObjectiveArrow`, `Wallet`), plus `Oven`, `ShopCounter`, `CustomerQueue`, `Customer`, `OrderBubble`, `MoneyCounter`, `HudCoinFlight`, `Hud`, `IslandCamera`, `UnlockPad`, `Game`.
* M1. Physics interpolation is on, so everything moved in `_process` (stack items, flying items, customers, bubbles, camera rig, arrow) has `physics_interpolation_mode = OFF`, and the stack reads the baker through `get_global_transform_interpolated()`. Without this the tower jittered against the interpolated body.
* M1. Stack dials: capacity 6, item_height 0.13, follow_k_bottom 40, follow_k_top 11, pickup 0.3 s apex 0.8. Measured at 6 m/s with 6 donuts: bottom lag 0.142 m, top lag 0.55 m, back to 0.0 m 0.8 s after stopping.
* M1. Pitch ladder follows the chain of pickups (step 0.06, reset after 0.6 s) instead of the stack count, as the reference suggests for trays.
* M1. Animation: one `AnimationTree` per character scene with a saved blend tree (`assets/anim/bunny_tree.tres`, `pirate_tree.tres`): `Loco` blend space by speed, `Hold` blend space for the carrying clips mixed by `Carry`, one shots `Shot_Yes`, `Shot_HitReact`, `Shot_Wave`, `Shot_No` (fade in 0.25 s). Holding blend 0.75 s; locomotion blend 0.3 s (exported), because a 0.75 s idle to run blend slid the feet at 6 m/s.
* M1. The clips import without loops; `CharacterAnim._ready` sets `LOOP_LINEAR` on the locomotion clips in memory, so the import settings stay at their defaults.
* M1. The old Quaternius OBJ packs (food, interior) store `Kd` in linear space and import far too dark (white 0.36). Corrected colours are saved materials in `materials/pal/` assigned as surface overrides on the instances; the source files are untouched. The oven `Glass` surface is a dark window with a warm orange emission.
* M1. Regular customers are Anne, Henry, Sharky and Mako. Captain Barbarossa is only the rare customer, so he reads as special.
* M1. Oven scale 0.75 (0.76 m wide, 1.21 m tall) instead of 0.55: at 0.55 the ovens were smaller than the donuts on the rack. Rack is `Shelf_Small1` at 0.75 beside the oven, 4 by 1 by 2 slots (capacity 8).
* M2. Camera: yaw is minus 30 degrees (camera south west, looking north east), which puts the kitchen upper left and the dock upper right as in Concept 1. Pitch 48, FOV 38 as stated. Distance 11.5 m. With pitch 48 and FOV 38 the horizon is above the frame; the concept's horizon line at 9 percent needs a far wider lens than the stated FOV, so the stated numbers win.
* M2. Light after the first rounds: ACES, exposure 0.8, white 6, ambient 0.42 (sky contribution 0.35), sun 1.15 at elevation 55 from the upper left, glow additive 0.45 with threshold 0.95, saturation 1.18, contrast 1.05. Exposure 1.0 with sun 1.3 washed the sand to cream through ACES.
* M2. Sand is one shader surface: dry, light patches and a wet rim by world height. Water is one plane: vertical depth colour, a foam band by vertical depth (constant width at any view angle), a drifting ripple, facets and glints. `sea_time` is a shader global written by the `Sea` node.
* M2. Pads sit south west of the stall (three shared spots A, B, C) off the oven to counter path: a pad on that path drained coins by accident in the first capture.
* M2. Layout after concept round 1: the stall cluster is compact and sits east of the centre near the dock, as Concept 1 frames it. Kitchen line at (5, -7) facing south, ovens at x -0.9, 1.5, 3.9, counter at (8.4, -0.4) rotated -52 degrees with the queue running south east behind the rope, pads at three shared spots south west of the stall: A (0.5, 0), B (3.6, 3.2), C (-2.6, 2.6). The west half of the island is kept for the tables, the statue and the house plot.
* M2. Camera distance 12.5 m (camera at 9.29 up, 8.36 back on the rig). Bunny alone is about 19 percent of the frame height, the bunny with a 10 donut tower about 28 percent.
* M2. Light round 3: ambient is a warm colour (1.0, 0.92, 0.84) at 0.55 instead of the sky, saturation 1.25. Sky ambient turned every shadow blue grey; the concept shadows are warm. Sand measured 250, 227, 162 against the concept's 237, 218, 143 to 252, 204, 116.
* M2. Cliffs use a flat warm grey override (`materials/cliff_rock.tres`) through wrapper scenes: the pack cliffs import dark maroon and read as holes at this exposure.
* M2. Customers enter and leave by the dock end of the beach (spawn and exit markers in the counter scene).
* M2. The final purchase pad will stand at the foot of the north west cliff and the house pops in on the cliff top: the cliff top is not walkable.
* M3. Concept round 4 (Concept 2): the concept island is a compact diorama that the stall fills. The plateau is now 27 by 21 m (ellipse 13.5 by 10.5) instead of the GDD's 36 by 26 m, centred on the stall: at 36 by 26 the stall was a small cluster on a big empty beach and the comparison read as another game. The one to one rule wins over the size.
* M3. Final island layout (metres): kitchen line at z -6 (ovens at x -5.9, -3.5, -1.1), counter at (3.4, 0.6) rotated -52 degrees, pad spots A (-4.5, 1.0), B (-1.4, 4.2), C (-7.6, 3.6), statue at (-9.2, -1.4), tables at (-6.4, 6.6) and (-1.6, 7.9), dock from x 12.2 at z -0.5, house pad at (-6.2, -3.4).
* M3. Pads reveal what they buy through groups (`reveal_<pad_id>`): the targets are placed hidden in `main.tscn` (or inside the counter scene for the display case) and the pad disables them (`process_mode` DISABLED, so their collision and zones are out of the world) until bought.
* M3. Two pads are offered at once, as the concept shows. Pad order and prices as the GDD table.
* M3. Tables reuse the counter script (`TableSet extends ShopCounter`): same serve zone cadence and cash tray, seated customers instead of the queue front. Seated pirates use `Idle` with the model lowered 0.22 m onto the chair (the pirate rigs have no sitting clip) and eat for 5 s before leaving.
* M3. The helper carries 4 donuts per trip, picks the oven of the flavor the front customer wants, and steps sideways if blocked for 0.5 s. Measured alone for 60 s: 24 donuts sold, 121 m walked, longest standstill 6.5 s.
* M3. Backpack: a native box on the bunny's back that changes tint with each capacity pad (teal, orange, purple).
* M3. Pad holograms: the OBJ oven and table with the hologram material; the skeleton, bunny, ship, house and cannon as wrapper scenes with every mesh overridden (`scenes/props/holo/`).
* M4. The ship moves in `_process` on the water plane with no physics body (rigid on screen); the island is an ellipse it cannot cross and the islets are circles. Top speed 9 m/s, 1.2 s to full, reverse 2 m/s, turn 55 degrees per second scaled by speed, bank 6 degrees, bob from the same wave formula as the water shader (`Sea.height_at`).
* M4. `Ship_Large` at 0.8 (12.3 m), bow rotated to the ship's forward. The bunny at the helm is a second authored bunny on the stern deck; the baker herself is hidden and disabled at the dock, so the back stack stays on the island.
* M4. Sea camera: offset 3.2 right, 8.2 up, 12.5 behind in the smoothed heading frame, looking 9 m ahead, FOV 50, yaw smoothing k 3, pulls back 15 percent at top speed. Island to sea and back is one 0.45 s tween of position, rotation and FOV on a blend camera.
* M4. Docking: `E` within 18 m of the mooring, or sailing back within 7.5 m after having left by 22 m. Soft ring from 122 m to 140 m turns the ship home; light depth fog only at sea (density 0.0035).
* M5. Cannons: aim 6 to 34 m, 60 degree half arc per side, 5 balls, reload 0.8 s (0.5 s and two balls per click with the double cannons). Ball flight 0.35 to 1.1 s on an arc; a hit is any enemy within its radius plus 1.2 m of the splash.
* M5. Shark: 2 hits, circles at 15 m, warns 1 s with a red ring, charges at 13 m/s with a leap, 10 damage, drops 15 to 25 coins. Tentacle: 3 hits, rises ahead of the ship, warns 1.2 s with a shadow, slams 7 m, 15 damage, drops a chest worth 60 to 100. Three sharks and two tentacles are kept alive around the island, sharks respawn behind the camera.
* M5. Loot within 4.5 m flies to the ship and joins the hold; docking unloads it as coin flights from the ship along the dock to the HUD counter.
* M6. Kraken: wakes on the departure after a voyage that unloaded 150 or more, 26 m from the dock. Three dark tentacles (scale 0.5, 6 hits each) attack in turn every 4.5 s; the storm grade drops brightness to 0.72, saturation to 0.75 and the sun to half with a teal tint over 0.8 s. The golden chest holds 900.
* M6. The Captain's Donut House pops in on the west cliffs (`Environment_House1` at 0.55) with a flag that climbs its pole; its pad stands at the foot of the kitchen side.
* M6. Title: the live island with five hard cut shots (orbit of the stall, dock dolly, high island, low counter, kitchen push in), 4.5 to 5 s each. Project theme (`assets/ui/theme.tres`): cream buttons with a brown outline, radius 44, font 50, minimum height 100.
* M7. Audio: every sound is baked offline by `tools/bake_audio.py` (numpy synthesis, no samples): `island.ogg` 57.6 s, `sea.ogg` 47 s, `kraken.ogg` 48 s, `ambience.ogg` 32 s, 33 effects. Loops are folded at the seam and marked looping at runtime. Music player at -7 dB, ambience at -14 dB on the Music bus; unlocks duck the music 9 dB for 1.2 s.
* M7. Economy retune after the first bot runs. With the GDD's starting numbers (arrivals every 4 s and 3 s, pads totalling 4,720) a full run took more than 8 minutes: customer arrivals capped income at about 4 coins per second. Final numbers: arrivals every 2.5 s at the start and 1.8 s with the display case, table arrivals every 7 s, pads 40, 80, 70, 120, 250, 150, 200, 150, 400, 200, 250, 1,000 (total 2,910; the concept's 250 statue pad kept), sale prices 6, 8, 10 as stated, Kraken 900, voyage loot 15 to 25 per shark and 60 to 100 per tentacle.
* M7. Measured by `tests/game/bot_full_run.gd` (real movement input on the island, ship autopilot at sea, 3x time scale): donuts on the back at 3.1 s, oven2 1.9 s, display case 18.7 s, backpack 38.7 s, oven3 63.7 s, statue 104.2 s, tables 126.0 s, helper 167.6 s, oven speed 171.1 s, ship 203.2 s, backpack 2 308.8 s, double cannons 313.9 s, house 323.3 s. Two voyages, 11 sharks and 5 tentacles down, 240 donuts sold, 3,540 coins earned.
* M7. Flavor deadlock fix: a carrier standing at the counter with a stack that holds none of the wanted flavor used to block the line until the customer's patience ran out (the helper and the bot both hit it with a full stack). After 1.6 s the front customer now changes its order to the flavor on top of the stack, with a bubble pop and a grumpy grunt, and pays that flavor's price.
* M7. The boarding ring keeps asking every 0.5 s while the baker stands on it, so standing there while the ship is still sailing in boards as soon as it moors.
